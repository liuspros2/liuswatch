OPPENHEIMER

Christopher Nolan's most ambitious film yet

Drama / History · 2023 · 3h 1min · By CineGuide Editors
9.2
CineGuide Score
⭐ 8.9
TMDB Rating

Nolan has made an atomic bomb of a film — and we mean that as the highest possible compliment. Oppenheimer is a three-hour intellectual thriller that never once loses your attention. Cillian Murphy delivers a performance of extraordinary restraint, and the IMAX sequences of the Trinity test may be the most viscerally terrifying thing ever committed to film without a single drop of blood.
