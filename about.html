Who we are

ABOUT US

Built for film lovers, by film lovers.

CineGuide is a free movie discovery platform that helps audiences worldwide find, explore, and legally watch films across every genre and era. We index hundreds of thousands of films — from Hollywood blockbusters to Nollywood gems — and guide you to the legal platforms where you can stream or download them.

We do not host, store, or distribute any video content. Every "Watch" button on our site links to a licensed, legal streaming or download platform. Our mission is to make legal movie discovery simple, beautiful, and accessible — especially for African audiences who deserve better than piracy-dependent sites.

All movie data, posters, and ratings are sourced from The Movie Database (TMDB) under their free API licence.

HOW IT WORKS

How we make money

CineGuide is funded by two sources: Google AdSense (display advertising shown on our pages) and affiliate commissions from streaming platforms. When you click a "Watch on Netflix" or "Buy on Amazon" link and make a purchase or sign up, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Our movie data

Film information, posters, ratings, and cast data are provided by TMDB via their free API. We do not own this data. TMDB is a community-built database similar to Wikipedia for movies.

Our reviews

Reviews and editorial content are written by our team of film enthusiasts. We do not accept payment for positive reviews. All opinions are our own.
